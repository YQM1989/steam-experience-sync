License: AGPL-3.0-or-later; full text in NXAPI-AGPL-LICENSE.txt.
The published CLI remains a separate background process. It is installed from
the pinned npm package with its original metadata and client authentication.
src/switch/nxapi-preload.mjs is an AGPL-3.0-or-later storage/privacy adapter.
It keeps Nintendo credentials in memory/OS credentials storage and disables
the album command's unrelated friend/chat/service/event/announcement calls.
Other dependency licenses are included in tools/nxapi-client/node_modules.
This is a local integration; public distribution requires a review of the
combined distribution and corresponding-source requirements.
